Importance of a Healthy Environment

A healthy environment is crucial for human well-being and is a foundation for numerous human rights, including the rights to life, health, and food. It directly impacts physical and mental health, contributing to reduced mortality, healthier lifestyles, and increased productivity. Furthermore, a healthy environment supports ecosystem services that are vital for human and animal health, including clean air, water filtration, and erosion prevention.
