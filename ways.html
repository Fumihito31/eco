Easy Ways to Help the Earth

  • ∘ Bring your own bag and water bottle
  • ∘ Reduce the usage of plastic
  • ∘ Turn off the lights when you leave a room
  • ∘ Unplug things when you're not using them
  • ∘ Take short showers and don’t waste water
  • ∘ Use things again instead of throwing them away
  • ∘ Plant a tree or a flower
  • ∘ Pick up trash when you see it
  • ∘ Talk to your friends and family about helping the Earth
  • ∘ Help at clean-up days or nature events
